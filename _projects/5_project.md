## Related Works

<br>


`1. Google DeepMind Veo 3 (text→video) - U-Net Diffusion + Cross-frame Attention`

<br>

Veo 3 employs a U-Net diffusion backbone conditioned on CLIP text embeddings, with cross-frame attention modules inserted at each U-Net layer to model temporal coherence over a XX-frame context.
While this improves short-range consistency, the quadratic cost of attention limits its effectiveness in long-shot scenarios, often leading to character identity drift.

<br>

`2. Runway Gen-4 (text→video) - Latent Diffusion with VAE`

<br>

Gen-4 leverages a VAE to project high-resolution images into latent space, applies U-Net diffusion guided by classifier-free text prompts, and decodes back to the pixel space.
It excels at refining keyframes in terms of identity and style but lacks temporal modeling across shots due to the need for manual keyframe selection and isolated frame processing.

<br>


`3. Sora (text→video) - Streaming Latent Diffusion with Sliding-window Attention`

<br>

Sora applies Transformer-based denoising in a latent space pipeline, combined with sliding-window causal attention and block-wise decoding for real-time video generation.
It ensures short-term continuity but is constrained by limited window size, making it insufficient for ensuring consistency in long-range shot transitions.

<br>


`4. Mamba - State Space Model (Sequence → Sequence, universal module for sequence modeling)`

<br>

Mamba-SSM integrates lightweight state-space blocks into U-Net cross-attention layers (parallel or in place of original memory modules). The discrete unfolding K_k=CA ̅^k B ̅ yields convolutional kernels that capture long-range dependencies in linear time for sequence-to-sequence modeling in latent space.
This enables linear-time modeling of long-range dependencies in latent space, significantly improving cross-shot consistency and reducing flicker without retraining or re-rendering.

<br>

`5. Black Forest Labs (image→image)`

<br>

Flow Matching for In-Context Image Generation and Editing in Latent Space, Based on latent flow matching generated by guided images, visual context consistency is constructed. 
Excellent performance in image editing and a small number of frames, good support for identity/style context consistency. Not suitable for long video temporal modeling.

<br>
