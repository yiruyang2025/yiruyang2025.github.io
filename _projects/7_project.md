## Tokenization

```
In multimodal generation, tokenization extends beyond text: images, speech, and music can be represented either as discrete codebook indices, as in VQ-VAE, SoundStream, and EnCodec, or as continuous latent tokens, as in patch-based Transformers, latent diffusion, and flow-matching models. Our work follows the latter direction by treating music generation as continuous perceptual tokenization, where pitch, rhythm, timbre, and phrase-level structure are preserved as continuous trajectories rather than being forced into a fixed discrete vocabulary.
```

## Tokenization

| Stage                                    | Dominant Assumption                                                                                   | Token Type                                           | Failure Mode                                                                                                           | Representative Methods                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Rule-based tokenization**              | Words or punctuation-delimited units are meaningful symbols.                                          | **Discrete hand-defined tokens**                     | Fails on OOV words, morphology, multilingual text, and scripts without whitespace.                                     | Whitespace splitting, WordPunct, dictionary segmentation                                   |
| **Subword tokenization**                 | Frequent character patterns form reusable linguistic units.                                           | **Discrete learned subword tokens**                  | Still imposes fixed segmentation and may fragment rare, multilingual, or domain-specific terms poorly.                 | BPE, WordPiece, Unigram LM                                                                 |
| **Language-agnostic tokenization**       | Raw text should be tokenized without language-specific preprocessing.                                 | **Discrete raw-text or byte-level tokens**           | Vocabulary statistics still depend on corpus distribution and tokenizer design.                                        | SentencePiece, byte-level BPE                                                              |
| **Systems-optimized tokenization**       | Tokenization must be fast enough for large-scale training and inference.                              | **Discrete production token IDs**                    | Compression efficiency, semantic granularity, and throughput can conflict.                                             | tiktoken-style optimized BPE tokenizers                                                    |
| **Learned discrete neural tokenization** | Continuous signals can be compressed into learned discrete codebook indices.                          | **Discrete neural codec tokens**                     | Quantization can lose fine details, introduce codebook collapse, or separate token efficiency from perceptual quality. | VQ-VAE, VQGAN, SoundStream, EnCodec                                                        |
| **Hierarchical neural tokenization**     | High-level semantic structure and low-level acoustic detail should be represented separately.         | **Mostly discrete multi-level tokens**               | Requires complex pipelines and can accumulate errors between semantic and acoustic stages.                             | AudioLM, MusicLM-style pipelines, codec language models                                    |
| **Patch / latent tokenization**          | Images, audio, and music can be modeled as sequences of learned latent vectors.                       | **Continuous vector tokens**                         | Longer-range structure may still require explicit hierarchy or conditioning.                                           | ViT, MAE, DiT, latent diffusion, latent flow models                                        |
| **Tokenizer-free modeling**              | Fixed token boundaries should be removed or minimized.                                                | **Low-level discrete bytes or continuous patches**   | Sequence length becomes much longer, increasing compute cost.                                                          | Byte-level LMs, character LMs, tokenizer-free Transformers                                 |
| **Continuous music tokenization**        | Musical structure is better represented as continuous perceptual trajectories than hard discrete IDs. | **Continuous perceptual / symbolic / latent tokens** | Requires careful evaluation beyond reconstruction loss, including rhythm, timbre, style, novelty, and long-range form. | Continuous-token music generation, flow-matching DiT / xiT, perceptual audio latent models |
