## SIGGRAPH

<br>

  - [2000 - A three-dimensional computer model of the human heart for studying cardiac fluid dynamics](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/563788.604453)
  - [2015 - Multi-scale, multi-physics heart simulator, UT-heart VFX breakdown](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/2745234.2746904), “Best Visualization or Simulation” at SIGGRAPH 2015’s Computer Animation Festival

<br><br>

| Year     | Title                                                                                                                                                                                          | Summary                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **2000** | *A Three-Dimensional Computer Model of the Human Heart for Studying Cardiac Fluid Dynamics* (SIGGRAPH)                                                                                         | Pioneering work by McQueen & Peskin using the Immersed Boundary Method to model full heart fluid dynamics; foundational for future fluid–structure interaction studies.                                         |
| **2015** | *Immersed boundary-finite element model of fluid-structure interaction in the aortic root* (Griffith et al.)                                                                                   | Combines IB method with finite-element hyperelastic modeling of the aortic root and valve; reproduces realistic cardiac output and valve mechanics.                                                             |
| **2017** | *Image-based immersed boundary model of the aortic root* (Hasan et al.)                                                                                                                        | Constructs anatomically realistic aortic root and ascending aorta from CT data, with fiber-reinforced leaflet mechanics; analyzes differences in valve material properties.                                     |
| **2019** | *Modeling the Mitral Valve* (Kaiser, McQueen, Peskin)                                                                                                                                          | Focuses on mitral valve modeling with design-based methodology and IB method; incorporates anatomy-informed fiber topology and realistic closure under physiological pressures.                                 |
| **2023** | *Comparison of Immersed Boundary Simulations of Heart Valve Hemodynamics against In Vitro 4D Flow MRI Data* (Kaiser et al.)                                                                    | Validates IB simulations against in vitro 4D flow MRI of a pulmonary valve; shows strong qualitative and quantitative agreement.                                                                                |
| **2023** | *Rapid model-guided design of organ-scale synthetic vasculature for biomanufacturing* ([Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adj6152))                                         | Introduces a computational pipeline for designing and fabricating synthetic, organ-scale vascular networks; validated with fluid dynamics simulations and 3D bioprinting, enabling scalable tissue engineering. |
| **2025** | *Scientists create vascularized mini-organs, advancing regenerative medicine* ([Stanford News](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2025/06/heart-liver-organoids-research-regenerative-therapy)) | Stanford researchers successfully grew vascularized heart and liver organoids, advancing regenerative therapies by creating mini-organs with their own blood vessel networks.                                   |

## 4d Medical Modeling

<br>

| Institution / Lab                                                              | Country          | Core Contribution                                                                                                                                                                             | Notes                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Courant Institute (NYU, Peskin & McQueen group)**                            | 🇺🇸 USA         | Introduced the **Immersed Boundary Method (IB)** and pioneered its use in cardiac fluid dynamics modeling; a mathematical and computational foundation of the entire field.                   | SIGGRAPH 2000, multiple landmark valve modeling papers           |
| **The Living Heart Project (Dassault Systèmes + FDA + multiple universities)** | 🇺🇸/🇪🇺 Global | World’s largest **virtual heart consortium**, providing full-organ cardiac modeling and cross-disciplinary simulation platform; partnered with the FDA for drug/device regulatory evaluation. | >150 institutions involved                                       |
| **UT-Heart Project (University of Tokyo)**                                     | 🇯🇵 Japan       | Multi-scale, multi-physics cardiac modeling; won **Best Visualization at SIGGRAPH 2015**; developed electro-mechanical-hemodynamic coupled UT-Heart simulator.                                | One of the world’s most detailed heart supercomputer simulations |
| **MIT Bioengineering + CSAIL (Polina Golland, Ellen Roche, et al.)**           | 🇺🇸 USA         | Image-guided cardiac modeling, soft robotic heart pumps, and AI-driven cardiac reconstruction.                                                                                                | Collaborations with MGH and Boston Children’s Hospital           |
| **Stanford Cardiovascular Institute**                                          | 🇺🇸 USA         | Imaging, valve modeling, precision cardiac digital twins; integrates **AI + HPC** for cardiovascular disease prediction.                                                                      | Partnered with NVIDIA for GPU acceleration                       |
| **ETH Zurich + University Hospital Zurich (Katzschmann, Dzemali, et al.)**     | 🇨🇭 Switzerland | Recent (2024–25) work on **4D cardiac patches (RCPatch)**, combining **volumetric printing + melt-electrowriting + generative modeling**.                                                     | Published in *Advanced Materials* 2025                           |
| **King’s College London – Biomedical Engineering**                             | 🇬🇧 UK          | Applied **4D Flow MRI + AI** for cardiac modeling; leading European center for imaging-computation integration.                                                                               | Collaborations with Philips and Siemens Healthineers             |
| **Harvard Wyss Institute**                                                     | 🇺🇸 USA         | Developed multiple **Organs-on-Chips**, pioneering the organ chip direction.                                                                                                                  | Closely affiliated with Emulate, Inc.                            |
