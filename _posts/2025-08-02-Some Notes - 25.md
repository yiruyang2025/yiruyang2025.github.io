## 4d Medical Modeling

<br>


[Emulate, Inc.](https://emulatebio.com/) - Organs‑on‑Chips

  - Simulates the functions of various human organs for drug development and toxicology testing
  - Collaborates with Cedars-Sinai Medical Center to advance precision medicine applications and conducts joint research with the US FDA to enable its "Human Emulation System" for regulatory evaluation

<br>

[BiomimX](https://www.biomimx.com/) - Beating Organs‑on‑Chip
  - heart, liver, muscle models
  - “uBeat™” platform for simulating cardiovascular and muscle tissue

<br>

[Mimetas](https://www.mimetas.com/en/applications/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=19940738854&gbraid=0AAAAADjrks_yt6LB-lDxzJWtjODsFmOia&gclid=Cj0KCQjwzaXFBhDlARIsAFPv-u9wFYixAsVfkD6LUf7I1IWg-FyE70ZcrzfwW6fJ-OhbZJhXjSW3GewaAnciEALw_wcB) - Complex tissues, in a relevant in vitro microenvironment

  - Development of the OrganoPlate (microfluidic multi-well chip) for high-throughput screening, supporting multiple tissue/disease models
  - Collaboration with pharmaceutical giants (Roche, Pfizer, etc.) for the application of 3D organ models in drug testing
