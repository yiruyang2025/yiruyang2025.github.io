## Post-Training Techniques ❄️ 

1. Parameter-Efficient Adaptation
   - Adapter
   - LoRA / QLoRA
   - Prefix-tuning
   - Prompt-tuning
   - BitFit

2. Model Compression and Deployment
   - Knowledge Distillation
   - Quantization
   - Pruning
   - Low-rank factorization
   - Sparse inference

3. Alignment and Preference Optimization
   - Supervised Fine-Tuning, SFT
   - Instruction Tuning
   - RLHF / RLAIF
   - DPO / IPO / KTO
   - Constitutional AI-style alignment

4. Model Manipulation
   - Model Editing
   - Model Merging
   - Task Arithmetic
   - Adapter Fusion
   - Weight Averaging

5. Continual / Domain Adaptation
   - Continual Learning
   - Domain Adaptation
   - Test-Time Adaptation
   - Online Adaptation

6. Data-Efficient Learning, related but not always post-training
   - Semi-Supervised Fine-Tuning
   - Self-Training
   - Pseudo-labeling
   - Few-shot adaptation
